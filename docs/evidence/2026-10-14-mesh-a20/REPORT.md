## A0 — acquisition order belongs to an independent stream

Baseline `0c5acbe` contains the preceding a3 source plus the execution plan.
Two owned Service 4 IPC tests fail against that clean archive with valid
original receipts: delayed status after a newer watch raises
`service_acquisition_superseded`; delayed watch baseline after newer status is
discarded and times out. No native gap, restart, expired lease or provider
activity is needed. Initial fixture drafts with counters exceeding attempts
were rejected by schema validation and are excluded from this proof.

The correction keeps one history per watch and a separate serialized status
history. An unrelated later completion cannot suppress a valid initial watch
or reject an independently sampled cached reply. Status serialization includes
acquisition and bounded queue time, not just admission. Pending reads and open
channels are bounded; histories retire with their operations.

Global source/component binding, highest known receipt metadata and bounded
incarnation retirement remain shared. Equal highest counters cannot renew their
original sample/expiry across streams. Regressions within a history, clock
regression, changed scope and retired publishers still reject atomically.
Original clocks/receipts are returned unchanged; independent observations do
not claim global completion order or latest-daemon timing.

Delayed first replies also recheck the socket inode/ownership before admission.
A replaced endpoint cannot become current through an older still-open socket,
including when its previous service ID has not yet been admitted. The separate
known-incarnation test primes the earlier service before proving retirement;
UUID order supplies no authority. These are owned fixtures, not native restart
or physical-suspend proof.

Focused coverage includes both delay orders, two independent watches, genuine
receipt/clock/scope regression, cross-stream renewal rejection, known retirement,
socket replacement, queued cancellation/deadline/capacity and current Agent
factory snapshot/watch/proof behavior. The optional package dependency floor
is Mesh a20 for the corrected publisher semantics; SDK/profile capability and
provider support remain contract-based. API 4/read 6/Service 4/profile v3,
preparation/read 1 and core/local-service Mesh independence are unchanged.
