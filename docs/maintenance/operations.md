# Releases and operations

The [0.7.0a12 prerelease](https://github.com/byebyebryan/agent-observer/releases/tag/v0.7.0a12)
adds the configured sessions CLI and terminal JSON interface 1. Its reader
installation/configuration is independently selected; publishing it does not
upgrade a collector or publisher. See the [sessions guide](../guides/sessions-cli.md).

The collector/working-set cohort retains the
[a11 clock-recovery pair](https://github.com/byebyebryan/agent-observer/releases/tag/v0.7.0a11)
with Mesh a25: Codex-only read-only API 4 / snapshot 6 / Service 4 and working-set
v1 interfaces. Full publishers, dashboards, Tmux and SSH authority retain
independent reviewed selections.

## Selection and recovery

The deployment owner maintains
[agent-observer.json](https://github.com/byebyebryan/dotfiles/blob/main/deployments/agent-observer.json).
Use its exact role artifacts and scoped operations, retain paired rollback
inputs, and verify installed bytes/readiness/recovery separately from source CI.
Preserve directional routes, existing native work and independent factories.
Installation, service hosting, collector selection and consumer adoption are
separate steps. No optional transport is selected by installing a package.

Observer writes and standalone active-only hosting are retired. Working-set
retains active membership plus recent context; full diagnostics remain available.
Earlier delivery receipts recorded Carbon membership as partial without a primary
cause. Current working-set reads expose a primary census, support or membership
blocker through `coverage.reason`; see [working-set reads](../guides/working-set.md).
Complete native inventory and complete working-set membership remain separate
claims. The previous a10 policy can leave quiet rows unresolved indefinitely
after a clock change, even when later source coverage is complete. The a11
recovery policy uses [bounded clock uncertainty](../guides/working-set.md#clock-changes-and-recovery)
instead of that permanent veto. Installing source does not update selected
collectors; qualification and scoped delivery remain separate gates.

## Provenance

The [a12 CLI manifest](../../artifacts/observer-0.7.0a12.json) binds runtime source
`44f9774`, the exact reader wheel and unchanged Mesh a25 bytes. Its public
[provenance](https://github.com/byebyebryan/agent-observer/releases/download/v0.7.0a12/provenance.json)
binds qualification/tag source `e008ec9`, all six successful CI jobs, Python
3.11/3.14 source/installed checks and cached consumer/terminal qualification.
All eight public assets were downloaded and matched to the qualified bytes.
The added terminal v1 schema is a consumer output contract; native producer
schemas and a11 clock-recovery behavior are unchanged. The persistent dashboard
is not part of this release. Managed CLI selection belongs to the independent
`deployments/agent-observer-cli.json` role in dotfiles; collector/publisher
selection remains in the producer manifests below.

The [a11 artifact manifest](../../artifacts/observer-0.7.0a11.json) binds the
clock-recovery runtime source, exact Observer wheel and existing Mesh a25 pair.
The release preserves API 4, snapshot/watch 6, Service 4 and working-set v1
schemas. Qualification covers the boot-step-shaped regression, quiet recovery,
native-turn identity joins and installed older-reader admission. These are
synthetic/package checks; no live clock was changed and no collector was
restarted as proof. The public release's
[provenance](https://github.com/byebyebryan/agent-observer/releases/download/v0.7.0a11/provenance.json)
binds runtime source `6d6652c`, qualification/tag source `9a14051`, the unchanged
qualified wheel and all six successful CI jobs. All seven public assets were
downloaded and matched against reviewed bytes before managed selection.
The manifest's candidate note records qualification-time status; publication
and deployment are established by their separate receipts. The
[a10 manifest](../../artifacts/observer-0.7.0a10.json) remains prior-artifact
provenance and rollback material.
