returns the actual fresh tag; changed native binding/context reports failure.

the accepted time-namespace spelling conversion provides no clock bridge.

nonce, incarnation, clocks and whole RTT/margin before any claim is current.

original absolute 15-second budget. Head has its separate two-second bound;
watch/channel lifetime is independent. Stopping a watch, catch-up, fallback and
one fresh proof retry cannot reset the acquisition budget. Proof performs no
body acquisition/stage or predecessor retirement. Authenticated nonce-echo hello
provides factory-minted bridgeId before setup; every reconnect changes channelEpoch
and a new factory changes bridgeId. No old admission crosses that binding.

Scope retirement revokes only its own refs/use/publication before awaited cleanup.
Late receive, seal, scan or write cannot publish; live I/O/raw/output pins retain
their credit until actual retirement. Live registries stay bounded, including
caller-retained consumed handles; metadata/header/copy leases are independent
from consumed data. Sessions never close a shared owner or sibling. The first
public protocol permits no cross-alias reseal or shared-credit shortcut: actual
independent copies are prepaid and zero-copy pins retain their original charge.
